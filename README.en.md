Draw a line and it comes alive, wriggling like hand-drawn animation.
Save your moving drawings as GIF or WebP and share them anywhere.

> [!NOTE]
> Ugurugu 3.0 is for Windows. On macOS, please keep using 2.2.13.
> `.ugu` files made in 2.x open in 3.0 as they are.

## Getting started

1. Pick a brush and draw.
2. Choose a motion in the **Wobble** panel and press `P` to play it.
3. Export a GIF or WebP from the **File** menu.

Press `F1` any time for help.

## What you can do

- **Wobble** — choose how strongly and how your drawing moves, and let each layer move or stay still.
- **Drawing** — pen, marker, airbrush and spray brushes with pen pressure, lines and shapes, selections and transforms, and text.
- **Pixel art** — draw crisp pixel art in pixel mode and export it enlarged.
- **Tabs** — work on several drawings in one window.
- **Make it yours** — arrange the panels, pick a light or dark theme and change any shortcut. If the app closes unexpectedly, your work comes back.

Settings are under **Edit → Settings** (`Ctrl+,`).

## Handy shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `B` / `E` | Brush / Eraser |
| `P` | Play / Pause |
| `Space` + drag, scroll | Move the canvas, zoom |
| `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y` | Undo / Redo |
| `I` or `Alt` + click | Pick a color |

You can change every shortcut in **Settings → Shortcuts**.

## If something goes wrong

Tell us what you were doing and what happened in a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/nyabi-gh/Ugurugu/issues). Attaching the
diagnostic info and logs from **Settings → About** helps us fix it quickly.
