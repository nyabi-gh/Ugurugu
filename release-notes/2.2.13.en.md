## Fixed

- On macOS, moving the pen or mouse over the canvas without drawing still
  left the brush ring where the pointer had entered the canvas. The ring now
  follows the pointer on macOS too.

## Improved

- Zooming out below 100% no longer stalls the canvas. The picture used to be
  redrawn at the new size right where you zoomed, freezing input for a moment
  each time you paused, and during playback for about a second while every
  frame was redrawn. The canvas now keeps showing the picture scaled while
  the new size is prepared in the background, then sharpens.
- Opening a picture while playback is on no longer makes the animation stutter
  for the first couple of seconds.
