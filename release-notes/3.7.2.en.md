Ugurugu 3.7.2 gives keys to 26 more actions, following Clip Studio Paint
and Photoshop, and lays out the buttons under a selection as Clip Studio
Paint's selection launcher. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to 3.7.1 offer the update by
themselves, or you can install it with the Setup file, disk image or
AppImage below.

## New

- Keys for actions that had none:
  - Layers: Ctrl+G adds a group, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups, Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[
    move the layer up and down, H shows or hides it and Ctrl+Delete
    deletes it.
  - File and edit: Ctrl+Shift+O inserts an image, Ctrl+Alt+W exports a
    WebP, Ctrl+Alt+I and Ctrl+Alt+C change the image and canvas size,
    Ctrl+Shift+B the canvas background, Ctrl+Shift+P turns pixel mode on
    and off, and Ctrl+Shift+R edits the strokes in the selection.
  - Selection and view: Shift+H and Shift+V flip the selection, 0 turns
    the canvas back straight and Ctrl+2 turns the symmetry ruler on and
    off.
  - Animation: Ctrl+Shift+A turns the animation tools on and off,
    Ctrl+Alt+F makes an animation folder and Ctrl+Alt+N a new cel.
  - Panels: F3 wobble, F4 color history, F5 tool settings, F6 color, F7
    layers and F8 navigator. A panel behind another tab or in a folded
    dock comes into view on the first press and closes on the next.

  Keys you chose yourself in the settings stay as they are.
- Invert selection and Fill selection have buttons under the selection.

## Changed

- The buttons under a selection are in the order of Clip Studio Paint's
  selection launcher: deselect and invert, delete and duplicate, transform
  and flip (with apply and cancel while transforming), then fill and edit
  strokes.
