Ugurugu 3.5.0 adds a navigator panel that shows the whole canvas and
moves the view, fixes brush previews that could stay blank after the
graphics driver restarted, and refuses damaged `.ugurugu` files whose
parts disagree with the file's index. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to 3.4.3 offer the
update by themselves, or you can install it with the Setup file below.

## New

- A navigator panel, as in Clip Studio Paint: Window > Navigator, or its
  tab behind Wobble. It shows the whole canvas with the part in view
  outlined; click it to bring that point to the middle of the view, or
  drag the outline to move the view. Sliders under it set the zoom and the
  rotation. (#133)

## Fixed

- After the graphics driver restarted, the brush previews and the app icon
  on the About tab could stay blank; they are drawn again at once. (#143)
- A `.ugurugu` file that lists one of its parts twice, or whose parts
  disagree with the file's index, is now refused as damaged. Before, it
  opened with whichever copy came last, which another ZIP tool might not
  have shown. (#147)
