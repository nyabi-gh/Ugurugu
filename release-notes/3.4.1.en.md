## Fixed

- A paint bucket or magic wand click made while an earlier one was still
  waiting for what it reads, as right after playback or an undo, was lost,
  and one answered while you had started drawing threw the stroke away.
  Clicks now end one after another in the order made, and one that comes in
  while you draw ends once the stroke is done. (#110)
- On a slow connection, below about 220 kbit/s, an update download stopped
  after ten minutes and started over. It now goes on from where it stopped,
  and gives up only when nothing comes in for two minutes. (#110)
- A stroke drawn back to where it started showed its end twice, a little
  apart, while it was drawn. It now shows as drawn and closes when the pen
  is lifted. (#110)
