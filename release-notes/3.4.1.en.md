## Fixed

- A paint bucket or magic wand click made while an earlier one was still
  waiting for what it reads, as right after playback or an undo, was lost,
  and one answered while you had started drawing threw the stroke away.
  Clicks now end one after another in the order made, and one that comes in
  while you draw ends once the stroke is done. (#110)
- On a slow connection, below about 220 kbit/s, an update download stopped
  after ten minutes and started over. It now goes on from where it stopped,
  and gives up only when nothing comes in for two minutes. (#110)
- A stroke drawn back to where it started showed its end twice, a little
  apart, while it was drawn. It now shows as drawn and closes when the pen
  is lifted. (#110)
- Ungrouping a clipped group that holds a layer clipped to another layer
  in it changed the picture: that layer came out clipped to the layer
  below the group. Such a group is now not ungrouped, and Ugurugu says
  why; turn the group's clipping off first to ungroup it. (#111)
- With the interface in Chinese, Ugurugu closed at every start when
  Windows' Microsoft YaHei font file could be read but not used. It now
  starts and shows the interface in its own font, which lacks some
  Chinese characters. (#111)
- A new pixel art document that could not be opened, as when the drawing
  shown had a transform that could not be applied, still changed the
  export size. (#111)
- New in a pixel art document filled in its canvas times the pixel size
  last chosen, even for a document made with another pixel size or opened
  from a file, and above 4096 the OK made a smaller canvas. New now starts
  from the pixel size the document was made with, and from its canvas
  when that is not known. (#111)
