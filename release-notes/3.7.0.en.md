Ugurugu 3.7.0 keeps the paint bucket inside lines that have a small break,
selects what a layer shows with a click on its thumbnail, lets the brush
draw with a transparent color, draws the sliders of the panels as one wide
bar and fixes six problems found in a review of the code. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to 3.6.4 offer the update by
themselves, or you can install it with the Setup file, disk image or
AppImage below.

## New

- The paint bucket and the magic wand no longer get out through a small
  break in the lines. "Close gaps" in their settings is the widest break
  they stop at, 2 px to start with and up to 20; at 0 they pass through
  every break as before. A passage about that narrow between two areas is
  filled up to its middle from each. (#207)
- Ctrl+click on a paint layer's thumbnail in the Layers panel selects what
  is drawn on that layer, where the frame shows it, without making it the
  current layer. A hidden layer's drawing is selected too. With Shift it is
  added to the selection, with Alt taken away from it. (#212)
- C turns the transparent color on and off; so does the checkered swatch
  beside the current color. With it the brush and the shape tool erase in
  their own shapes and sizes, where they would have painted. Choosing a
  color turns it off. The paint bucket and text keep the color. (#212)

## Changed

- The sliders of the tool settings, layer properties and wobble panels are
  now one bar as wide as the panel, with the name and the value inside it,
  in place of a short slider beside a number field. Drag along the bar to
  change the value, hold Shift to change it slowly, and click the bar to
  type a number. A press alone no longer changes the value. (#206)

## Fixed

- Changing between the pen and the symmetry ruler made the left dock a
  little wider each time. (#206)
- Undoing a change to a layer's outline colour put the colour back at once
  as a new step, and the opacity and outline width sliders kept showing
  the value from before the undo. (#204)
- Typing a number of frames such as 100 set it to 2 and 10 on the way,
  which removed the keys and cels past those frames. A value typed in a
  number field is now set when you press Enter or leave the field. (#204)
- A selection moved wholly off the canvas stayed visible where it had
  been, though exports and playback showed it gone. (#204)
- Dragging a layer with the move tool while the animation played was
  called off after a moment, and pressing the play key while drawing lost
  the stroke. Pressed during a stroke or a drag, the play key now starts
  or stops playing once that is over. (#204, #215)
- After a stroke drawn with the mouse, the brush ring showed where the
  stroke began until the mouse moved again.
- A drawing whose fills and selections used the whole 128 MiB a file may
  hold was saved but could not be opened again. (#204)
- Linux: starting the AppImage again, or opening a drawing with it, while
  a window was open did nothing. The open window now comes forward and
  opens the drawing. (#204)
