## New

- The interface is in Simplified Chinese too. It is chosen when Windows is
  set to Chinese (Simplified), or in Settings > General > Language, and uses
  Windows' Microsoft YaHei font. (#93)

## Fixed

- A layer clipped to the layer below disappeared when it was put in a
  group or an animation folder. The group or folder is now clipped in its
  place, ungrouping gives the clipping back, and a group's clipping can be
  turned off in the layer properties like a layer's.
- A copy from a layer moved with keys was pasted with its selection off by
  the move, so moving it cut out another part, and pasting it in a document
  with fewer frames failed. The paste is now selected where it is shown, and
  keys past the document's last frame are left out.
- Saving to a path longer than 260 characters failed after the file was
  written. Such files now save and save over.
- When the connection stalled, the update check never ended and later
  checks, the one in the Help menu too, waited for it, and a stalled
  download showed as downloading for ever. The check now gives up after a
  minute and the download after ten, and says so when asked.
- A click with the paint bucket or the magic wand right after playback, or
  right after an undo, held the window until the frames still waiting for
  playback were drawn: over a second on a large drawing. The window now
  stays responsive and the fill comes in as soon as what it reads is
  drawn.
