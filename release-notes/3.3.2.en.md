Ugurugu 3.3.2 makes the symmetry ruler a tool of its own that is placed
and moved on the canvas, lets the paint bucket fill on both sides of the
ruler, and checks for updates every time it starts. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to
3.3.1 offer the update by themselves, or you can install it with the
Setup file below.

## Improved

- The symmetry ruler is a tool of its own in the tool bar on the left
  (R), with its settings shown when it is chosen instead of under the brush
  and eraser. Drag on the canvas with it to lay the ruler's first line from
  where the drag starts, and drag its centre to move it. Hold Shift to
  turn the line in 15° steps. (#88)

## Fixed

- A new version was offered only when Ugurugu started six hours or more
  after its last check, so one started sooner said nothing. It now checks
  every time it starts, and every six hours while it stays open. (#91)
- With the symmetry ruler on, the paint bucket filled only the area
  clicked. It now fills the mirrored and turned areas too, in one fill that
  undoes in one step. (#89)
