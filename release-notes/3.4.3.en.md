## Improved

- On a screen too small for its usual size, such as a 1920 × 1080 laptop
  at 150%, Ugurugu opens maximized the first time, so that the panels have
  room. (#121)
- The Panels, Undo and Redo buttons are now at the right end of the menu
  bar instead of in a bar of their own, which gives the panels and the
  canvas more height. (#121)
- The Wobble panel shows pose count, detail, linked and randomness only
  for the Smooth and Stepped motion styles, and break amount and break
  range only when Broken line is on, instead of showing them dimmed.
  (#121)
- Panels beside the canvas keep their height: choosing another tool no
  longer moves the colour wheel up and down. The edge between two panels
  can be dragged, or moved with the arrow keys once it has the focus, to
  give one of them more room. (#122)
- In a short window the colour wheel gets smaller instead of going out of
  sight, and Tool settings shows the size, opacity and stabilization rows
  before the brush presets. (#122)
- The colour history is now a tab of the Color panel in the default
  layout; choose Window > Reset panel layout to get it. (#122)
