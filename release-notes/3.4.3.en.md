## Fixed

- A stroke drawn with an aliased pen, such as the ink pen of a fresh
  install, could show a pixel at its edge on the canvas that the exported
  image did not have, or miss one it had, until the canvas was drawn
  again. The canvas now shows a stroke as the export draws it. (#124)
- Edit > Canvas background… is back, as in 2.2.13: it sets the colour
  under every layer, and lowering its alpha makes the background
  transparent, which GIF and WebP exports can then keep. Drawings made in
  3.x were always on white paper until now. (#125)

## Improved

- On a screen too small for its usual size, such as a 1920 × 1080 laptop
  at 150%, Ugurugu opens maximized the first time, so that the panels have
  room. (#121)
- The Panels, Undo and Redo buttons are now at the right end of the menu
  bar instead of in a bar of their own, which gives the panels and the
  canvas more height. (#121)
- The Wobble panel shows pose count, detail, linked and randomness only
  for the Smooth and Stepped motion styles, and break amount and break
  range only when Broken line is on, instead of showing them dimmed.
  (#121)
- Panels beside the canvas keep their height: choosing another tool no
  longer moves the colour wheel up and down. The edge between two panels
  can be dragged, or moved with the arrow keys once it has the focus, to
  give one of them more room. (#122)
- In a short window the colour wheel gets smaller instead of going out of
  sight, and Tool settings shows the size, opacity and stabilization rows
  before the brush presets. (#122)
- The colour history is now a tab of the Color panel in the default
  layout; choose Window > Reset panel layout to get it. (#122)
- New Select, Layer and Animation menus. Selection and transform commands
  moved from Edit to Select, and the animation commands from View to
  Animation; the Layer menu has what the layer panel's buttons do.
- Layer commands, the brush size and the colour pair have keys:
  Ctrl+Shift+N adds a layer, Ctrl+J duplicates it, Ctrl+E merges it down,
  [ and ] make the brush smaller and larger, and X swaps the current and
  previous colours. Exporting a GIF moved from Ctrl+E to Ctrl+Alt+E, and
  a new cel no longer has a key. Keys you chose yourself stay as they were.
