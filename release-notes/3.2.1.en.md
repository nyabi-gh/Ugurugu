Ugurugu 3.2.1 makes a shape being dragged out follow the pointer instead
of lagging behind it. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to 3.2.0 offer the update by
themselves, or you can install it with the Setup file below.

## Fixed

- Shapes lagged behind the pointer while being dragged out, more so the
  larger the shape and the faster the mouse reports, and could stall for a
  moment before catching up. A shape being dragged now follows the pointer
  like a line being drawn. (#51, #72)
