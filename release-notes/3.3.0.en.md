## New

- The shape tool draws parallelograms, trapezoids, semicircles and
  crescent moons, and how far in a star's inner corners sit can be set.
  (#51, #77)
- Closed shapes can be filled: choose Outline, Fill or Both in the tool
  settings. The fill is in the pen's colour and does not wobble, like the
  paint bucket's; with Both the outline is drawn over it, in one step to
  undo. (#51, #77)

## Changed

- Dragging out a large shape takes less time each frame. (#51, #77)

## Fixed

- Switching tabs while dragging out a selection left the unfinished
  selection outline on the tab left behind. (#76)
- In a window too narrow for the side panels, pressing a slider or button
  in a panel saved the squeezed width as the panel's own; only dragging
  the panel's edge sets it now. (#76)
- The next and previous tab shortcuts, once changed, could not be set back
  to Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab without resetting every shortcut. (#76)
- Recovery folders left behind when a window stopped while starting, or
  when closing could not remove them, stayed on disk; a later start now
  tidies them. (#76)
