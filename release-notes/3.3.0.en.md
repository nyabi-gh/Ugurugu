## New

- The shape tool draws parallelograms, trapezoids, semicircles and
  crescent moons, and how far in a star's inner corners sit can be set.
  (#51, #77)
- Closed shapes can be filled: choose Outline, Fill or Both in the tool
  settings. The fill is in the pen's colour and does not wobble, like the
  paint bucket's; with Both the outline is drawn over it, in one step to
  undo. (#51, #77)
- Keys can fade a layer: the Move layer tool's Opacity is keyed with the
  rest of the pose. (#79)
- The point a layer turns and scales about starts in the middle of what
  it has drawn, and can be moved in the Move layer tool's settings, or put
  back in the middle of the drawing; the canvas marks it. (#79)
- A key can be copied and pasted onto another frame or layer from the Move
  layer tool's settings. (#79)
- Right-click the onion skin button to show up to four frames before and
  after, and to set how strongly they show. (#79)
- Animations can have up to 240 frames. Drawings with more than 60 frames,
  or with keys that fade, cannot be opened by Ugurugu 3.2 or earlier. (#79)

- Drawings Ugurugu opens or saves show in its taskbar Jump List, also
  those opened by dropping them on the window or saved with Ctrl+S. (#80)
- The animated export window says how long the animation plays, and that
  GIF drops soft transparency; exporting a transparent drawing as JPEG says
  that its transparent areas came out white. (#80)

## Changed

- Edit > Canvas size changes the canvas on the canvas itself: drag the
  frame's handles to grow or crop it and drag inside it to move it, and
  see what will be cut off and what will be added before applying. The
  size, reference point and offset can still be typed in a small window
  beside it, which can be moved. (#81)
- The onion skin updates faster while drawing: frames that did not change
  are not drawn again. (#79)
- Dragging out a large shape takes less time each frame. (#51, #77)

## Fixed

- Switching tabs while dragging out a selection left the unfinished
  selection outline on the tab left behind. (#76)
- In a window too narrow for the side panels, pressing a slider or button
  in a panel saved the squeezed width as the panel's own; only dragging
  the panel's edge sets it now. (#76)
- The next and previous tab shortcuts, once changed, could not be set back
  to Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab without resetting every shortcut. (#76)
- Recovery folders left behind when a window stopped while starting, or
  when closing could not remove them, stayed on disk; a later start now
  tidies them. (#76)
- Layers moved with the Move layer tool could not be merged down, even
  when they stay in one place on every frame, as copies of a layer put in
  other places do. They merge now, keeping where they are; layers that move
  from frame to frame still do not. (#78)
- A damaged file claiming millions of entries could take seconds and
  gigabytes of memory before being refused; it is now refused at once.
  (#80)
