Ugurugu 3.3.1 adds a symmetry ruler that draws every stroke again
mirrored or turned around a centre, and exports pixel art at its own size
or enlarged with each pixel a square block. Ugurugu 3.0.1 to 3.3.0 offer
the update by themselves, or you can install it with the Setup file below.

## New

- A symmetry ruler: turn it on in the View menu or in the brush and eraser
  settings, and every stroke is drawn again mirrored across its line, or
  around its centre with up to 12 lines. The centre, the lines' angle and
  whether strokes are mirrored or only turned are set there too. Each copy
  is a stroke of its own that wobbles by itself; all of them undo in one
  step. (#85)

## Improved

- In pixel mode, exporting an image asks for its size first, and it can be
  enlarged 2, 4 or 8 times with each pixel a square block. An animated GIF
  or WebP of pixel art now starts at its own size instead of being shrunk,
  and both remember the enlargement last chosen. (#24, #86)
