## New

- Drawings can move. With the new Move layer tool (V), drag the layer on
  one frame and again on a later one: each drag keys that frame, and the
  frames between follow, looping back to the first key, so two keys make
  a drawing go back and forth. A key can also turn, scale and flip the
  layer, and ease on smoothly or hold until the next key. Keys work on
  groups too, moving everything in them.
- Animation folders show one layer, a cel, per frame, for drawing each
  frame anew. Drawing on a frame without a cel adds one. The onion skin (O)
  shows the frames before and after faintly, in red and blue.
- The animation bar has rows for the keys and cels of the current layer.
  Comma and full stop step through frames, and with Shift through keys;
  K adds or removes a key and Ctrl+Shift+N adds a cel.
- Projects with keys or animation folders cannot be opened by Ugurugu 3.1
  or earlier, which says they were made by a newer version. Projects
  without them are saved as before.
- Layers can be duplicated again, as in 2.2.13: the new button after the
  group buttons puts a copy of the current layer, with its wobble and
  properties, right above it.

## Changed

- The layer buttons are clearer. Merging with the layer below has its own
  icon, an arrow down onto a line, instead of the same arrow as moving a
  layer down, and the buttons are grouped: making layers, duplicating and
  merging, and moving up and down. Deleting a layer is the trash can at the
  right end, apart from the others, instead of a "−" button among them.

## Fixed

- With wobble set to 0 px, some lines disappeared once drawn and the hard
  eraser removed nothing; both came back with any other wobble. Straight
  parts of a line no longer make the whole line vanish (#64).
