## New

- Layers can be duplicated again, as in 2.2.13: the new button after the
  group buttons puts a copy of the current layer, with its wobble and
  properties, right above it.

## Changed

- The layer buttons are clearer. Merging with the layer below has its own
  icon, an arrow down onto a line, instead of the same arrow as moving a
  layer down, and the buttons are grouped: making layers, duplicating and
  merging, and moving up and down. Deleting a layer is the trash can at the
  right end, apart from the others, instead of a "−" button among them.
