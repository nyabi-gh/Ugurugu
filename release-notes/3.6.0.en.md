## New

- Shift+click with the brush or the eraser draws a straight line from where
  the last stroke ended, as in Photoshop; drag on from there to keep
  drawing. While Shift is held, a thin line shows where it will go. The
  line wobbles along its length like a drawn one. (#157, #158)
- Alt+right-drag on the canvas changes the size of the brush or the
  eraser: right to grow, left to shrink. The ring stays where the drag
  began and shows the size. On macOS, Option+right-drag or
  Control+Option+drag does the same, the latter as in Photoshop for Mac.
  (#157, #158)
- Fade in the brush settings narrows a stroke from where it starts over the
  length you set, and ends it there, for hair and long tapered shapes. A
  document with such a stroke needs this version to open. (#157)
- Outline under a paint layer's properties draws a line around everything
  the layer shows, up to 250 px wide and in any color, as Stroke does in
  Photoshop's Blending Options. It follows the layer's wobble and shows
  while you draw. A document with an outline needs this version to open.
  (#157)
