## New

- Shift+click with the brush or the eraser draws a straight line from where
  the last stroke ended, as in Photoshop; drag on from there to keep
  drawing. While Shift is held, a thin line shows where it will go. The
  line wobbles along its length like a drawn one. (#157)
- Alt+right-drag on the canvas changes the size of the brush or the
  eraser: right to grow, left to shrink. The ring stays where the drag
  began and shows the size. On macOS, Option+right-drag or
  Control+Option+drag does the same, the latter as in Photoshop for Mac.
  (#157)
