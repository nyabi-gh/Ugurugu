## Fixed

- While a filled shape was dragged, its outline showed lighter where it
  lay over the fill when the pen was not fully opaque, and the fill's edge
  changed when the shape was placed. The shape now shows while dragged as
  it is placed. (#114)
- An edit made while dragging, such as one from a shortcut or a panel,
  stopped the drag but left a layer moved with the move tool half moved
  or a selection half made. Such an edit now ends the drag without its
  result, as Esc does. (#114)
- When the settings could not be saved, for example while another program
  held the file, nothing said so and they stayed unsaved until the next
  change. Ugurugu now says so in the status bar and keeps trying until they
  are saved. (#114)
- On a computer short of memory for a large drawing, the canvas could show
  colours up to two levels apart from the exported image where translucent
  layers lay over the one being edited. The canvas now shows the exported
  colours. (#115)
- With debug logging turned on, the log kept the text typed into the text
  tool. It now keeps only its length. (#114)
- The line under each choice in the settings of the selection, auto select
  and paint bucket tools, such as Pixel-based, was cut off at the panel's
  edge. It now wraps. (#117)
- New layers, groups, animation folders and cels were named in English, as
  "Layer 2", whatever the interface language, so a copy was named in two
  languages. They are now named in the interface's language, as in 2.2.13.
  (#117)

## Improved

- With the interface in Chinese, Ugurugu reads Windows' Chinese font while
  its window opens instead of after, which took about 6 ms. (#114)
- Turning the canvas left and right and flipping the view showed the icons
  of undo, redo and flipping the selection, and editing stroke properties
  showed the brush's. Each now has its own. (#117)
- Restore defaults in the settings, which also puts back every shortcut
  changed, the language and the theme, now asks first. (#117)
- The Wobble animation setting now says that turning it off also turns off
  the preview and animated GIF and WebP export. (#117)
- The English interface is written in sentence case throughout, and the
  Korean one uses one word for each thing it names. (#117)
